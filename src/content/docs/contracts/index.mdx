MIST's contracts are written in Solidity and built with Foundry. Three kinds of contract carry the protocol: the Chamber, Reserves and middlewares. Two more support them: the verifier and the registrar.

| Contract | Role | Entry points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Chamber` | Verifies each spend and applies it to state. Holds no assets | `deposit`, `handleZkp`, `registerReserve`, `setReserveScreener`, `approveDeposits`, `rejectDeposits`, `reclaimDeposit`, `setVerifier` |
| `Reserve` | Holds the assets of its notes under one rule set. Moves them only when the Chamber asks | `deposit` and `withdraw` (Chamber only), `setRegistrar`, `addUser` (registrar only), `updateAuditorKey` |
| `ChamberVerifier` | The Groth16 verifier, generated by gnark. Accepts compressed and uncompressed proofs and rejects any public input at or above the field order | `verifyProof` |
| `ManagerSigRegistrar` | The interim registrar for every Reserve. Anyone can submit a registration that carries the manager's signature | `registerUser(reserve, leaf, signature)` |

Middlewares are contracts that own notes. A note owned by a middleware is spent with `auth_done = 0`, so only the middleware can submit the spend, and it decides first whether the spend happens. None exists yet.

## The three flows

1. **Deposit.** `Chamber.deposit(reserve, ownerCommitment, amount, asset)` takes no proof. The depositor approves the Reserve, which pulls the asset. At a Reserve with a screener the note is queued, and the screener calls `approveDeposits` or `rejectDeposits`. The depositor can call `reclaimDeposit` while the deposit is pending or rejected.
2. **Transfer.** `Chamber.handleZkp(proof, publicInputs, auditorCommitments)` verifies the proof, checks the roots and the authorisation, marks both nullifiers spent and appends both new notes. No assets move.
3. **Withdrawal.** The same call with a non-zero `withdraw_amount`. The Chamber also calls `Reserve.withdraw`, which pays the recipient.

:::caution[Assets sent to a Reserve directly are lost]
A Reserve takes assets only through `Chamber.deposit`. Assets sent to a Reserve address by a plain transfer are unrecoverable.
:::

## Events

The Chamber emits `DepositQueued`, `DepositApproved`, `DepositRejected`, `DepositReclaimed`, `PrivateTransfer`, `ReserveScreenerSet` and `VerifierUpdated`. A Reserve emits `UserRegistered` and `RegistrarSet`.

An unscreened deposit emits nothing, and neither does a spend that publishes no commitments. Rebuilding the transactions tree therefore reads `getTxArray`, which returns the whole history.

## Toolchain

The contracts are pinned to solc 0.8.35, the Paris EVM and 200 optimizer runs. `ChamberVerifier.sol` is generated by gnark from the circuit and is never edited by hand.

## Source

The contracts are in `core/contracts/src`. The [specification](https://github.com/mistcash/mist/blob/main/spec/02_smart_contract.md) defines their state, actions and trusted roles.
