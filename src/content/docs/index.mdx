## What MIST is

MIST is a protocol for private payments of ERC-20 assets on EVM chains. Value lives in private notes. A note is a hash that commits to an amount of one asset, its owner and the Reserve that holds it. Nobody can read a note from the chain. Its owner proves facts about it in zero knowledge.

A spend consumes up to two notes and creates two new ones. It can also pay part of the value out to a public address. Observers see two nullifiers and two new note hashes. They do not see who was paid, which asset or how much.

## Separate custody, one shared record

Two kinds of contract split the work.

- A **Reserve** holds the assets of the notes in its custody and stands for one compliance rule set. Its manager decides who registers and who screens deposits.
- The **Chamber** keeps the record of every note, the spent markers and the list of Reserves. It verifies each proof and holds no assets.

Funds under different rules never mix. Private payments from every Reserve are recorded in the same Chamber, so they look alike.

## Compliance per Reserve

A Reserve can switch on three rules.

- **Registration.** At a Reserve with members, only registered users can spend.
- **Deposit screening.** A screener approves or rejects each deposit before its note joins the record. A depositor can always reclaim a deposit that is pending or rejected.
- **Manager visibility.** At a Reserve with members, every spend publishes its details encrypted for the manager: the sender, the asset and the amounts. Recipients appear only as commitments.
